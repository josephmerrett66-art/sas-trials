# Woodland details

From Quaternius Stylized Nature MegaKit Standard (CC0), provided locally by the user. `manifest.json` lists the 35 bundled models: three Rock_Medium shapes, RockPath_Round_Wide, and every small bush, fern, plant, clover, flower, mushroom, petal and pebble variant. The third medium rock is scaled larger in the game. Asset-pack grass is excluded; grass remains custom.

Models retain source geometry and diffuse textures. Unused normal/occlusion material bindings are removed; runtime converts PBR materials to Lambert and shares diffuse textures. Source dependencies are bundled locally. See LICENSE.txt.
