EXERCISE 01 · EIGHT CHECKPOINTS

Find your
own way.

Pack your bergen. Read the land. Choose your route.
Follow eight posts across the wilderness.
Each post reveals only the next coordinates.
Your map and compass are always in hand. Look down to read them.
Carry water and rations, or travel light and live off the land.
Days end. Gather wood, light a fire and camp through the night.
Set snares, cook what you catch, and keep warm.

Preparing the forest…

BEFORE YOU STEP OFF · KIT LIST

Pack your bergen.

Drag kit into the bergen, or tap it and tap a space. Turn a piece with R, a right click or Turn. Drag it out to unpack. Leave room: wood and game you collect have to fit too.
